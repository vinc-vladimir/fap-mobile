# Velocity Light Design System

## Brand Identity
**Name:** Velocity Light
**Primary Color:** #00F5FF (Velocity Cyan)
**Secondary Color:** #44474A (Deep Slate / Inactive)
**Surface Background:** #F7F9FB
**Surface Container:** #FFFFFF
**Font Family:** Inter (Sans-serif)
**Corner Radius:** 24px (Large), 12px (Medium), 8px (Small)

---

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** `#00F5FF` - Used for primary actions, active navigation states, and brand highlights.
- **Surface:** `#F7F9FB` - Main background color for all screens.
- **Surface-Container:** `#FFFFFF` - Background for cards, inputs, and modal areas.
- **On-Surface:** `#1A1C1E` - Primary text color for high contrast.
- **On-Surface-Variant:** `#44474A` - Secondary text and inactive icon states.
- **Outline:** `#74777A` - Border and divider colors.

---

## Typography
- **Header 1 (H1):** `Inter`, Bold, 24px. Used for main screen titles.
- **Header 2 (H2):** `Inter`, Semi-Bold, 20px. Used for section headers.
- **Body Text:** `Inter`, Regular, 14px-16px. Main content text.
- **Label Text:** `Inter`, Medium, 12px. Used for buttons and small labels.

---

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled background (`#00F5FF`), White bold text, 12px corner radius.
- **Secondary Button:** Light grey or white surface, icon-driven (e.g., Biometric Sign In), 12px corner radius.
- **Social Login:** Outlined buttons with brand icons (Google, GitHub), 12px corner radius.

### Input Fields
- **Standard Input:** White background (`#FFFFFF`), light grey border (`#74777A`), 12px corner radius. 2px Cyan outline on focus.

### Navigation
- **Top App Bar:** Clean, text-only or logo-driven (Velocity branding). Transparent or white surface.
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:**
  - **Tabs:** Home, Refuel, Activity, Account.
  - **Active State:** Cyan pill background (`#00F5FF`) with white icon.
  - **Inactive State:** Deep slate grey (`#44474A`) for icons and labels.
  - **Layout:** Fixed bottom, 4-tab structure, blur/glassmorphism effect where applicable.

### Cards
- **Container:** White surface, subtle shadow (0px 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)), 24px corner radius for main containers, 12px for internal cards.
